Your AI doesn’t write slop because it’s dumb.
It writes slop because it doesn’t know you.

It doesn’t know what you sell, who you serve, what you’d never put your name on, or how you actually talk. So it guesses. You rewrite it. You call the tool broken. You buy another tool.

Four Files is the missing brief.

You write four short pages, once:

  • Company — what you sell, who it’s for, how you make money
  • Customer — their words, their fears, why they buy
  • Offer — what’s in, what’s out, claims you will never make
  • Voice — examples of good and bad, not “friendly and confident”

Then you hand that folder to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, anyone. Same four files, every time. The model stops inventing a business you don’t run.

No login. Nothing leaves your browser. You download a zip and own it.

People pay two to five thousand dollars for someone else to build this layer. Four Files is the free desk where you write it yourself in an afternoon — and every AI you use after that finally sounds like you.
