### Challenge 1 — §3 Competition structure (unlimited pivot)

**Clause:** “Neither project is locked to its current form. Either side may pivot completely if evidence says it should.”

**Why it’s weak:** “Evidence” is undefined. That fights §8 anti-sprawl. A new name every month can look like progress.

**Replacement:**

> Neither project is locked forever. A pivot is allowed only when the side posts a short evidence note: what failed, what is killed or frozen, and what is tried next. Stefano can veto the pivot. No credit for a new product until the prior one is killed or frozen.

**Failure mode avoided:** infinite half-starts counted as winning.
